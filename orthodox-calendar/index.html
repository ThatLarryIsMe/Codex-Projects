The SecondLifeIcons Difference

Every piece we offer is a bridge to the ancient faith. We take pride in the meticulous restoration and digital refinement of historical art styles, breathing new life into classic mosaic and Byzantine depictions.

Our prayer cards and prints are crafted with a strict devotion to historical accuracy, ensuring that every Troparion and Kontakion honors the tradition—right down to highly researched Greek and liturgical source references.
